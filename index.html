Adrien Banse

Postdoctoral researcher at Automatic Control Laboratory, ETH Zurich

My research area is at the intersection of Mathematics and Computer Science. I am mainly interested in safe and robust decision-making in the presence of uncertainty, and more precisely in

  • Smart data-driven methods for abstracting dynamical systems, with guarantees
  • Analysis and control of adversarial switched linear systems, and their use to analyze optimization algorithms

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Automatic Control Laboratory at ETH Zurich, under the supervision of Prof. John Lygeros. I completed my Ph.D. at the Mathematical Engineering department of the ICTEAM, at UCLouvain, under the supervision of Raphaël Jungers (UCLouvain) and Alessandro Abate (University of Oxford). My Erdős Number is 4.
